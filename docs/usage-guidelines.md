# SportHub brand usage guidelines

## Brand character

SportHub is a softball-first coaching enablement platform for volunteer and inexperienced coaches. The identity should feel confident, encouraging, practical, and unmistakably connected to the field—not like a generic sports scoreboard.

**Approved tagline:** “Coach like you've done this before.”

Use the tagline as supporting copy, not as part of the logo. Keep product wording supportive and plain-spoken; avoid implying that a volunteer coach should already be an expert.

## Logo system

### Primary wordmark

Use `sporthub-wordmark.svg` on light surfaces. It uses Dugout Navy for “Sport” and Slate for “Hub.” It is the default brand mark for headers, documents, and product surfaces.

### Reversed wordmark

Use `sporthub-wordmark-reversed.svg` on Dugout Navy or another sufficiently dark approved surface. Do not place the reversed mark on Chalk or White.

### Softball product lockup

Use `sporthub-softball-lockup.svg` when the softball-first product identity needs to be explicit, such as launch materials and sports-specific documents. “SOFTBALL” is part of the lockup and should not be retyped or recolored independently.

### SH monogram

Use `sh-monogram.svg` and `sh-monogram-reversed.svg` only for compact contexts: app icon, favicon, navigation, or avatar-sized surfaces. Do not substitute the monogram for a full wordmark on large brand surfaces.

### Clear space and minimum size

- Keep clear space on every side equal to at least one quarter of the mark's width. Add this with layout padding; the SVGs do not include a built-in safety margin.
- Minimum display width: **120 px** for a wordmark/lockup.
- Minimum display size: **24 × 24 px** for the monogram.
- Use the supplied PNG derivative when a consumer cannot render SVG, and choose a size at least as large as the rendered output to avoid blur.

### Do not

- Stretch, skew, rotate, crop, outline, or add a shadow to a mark.
- Recreate the wordmark with a typed font or change its letter spacing.
- Add gradients or illustrations inside a logo mark.
- Use Optic Yellow for body-copy text or as a large background on Chalk.
- Use future-sport artwork as if it were an approved SportHub offering.

## Color palette

| Name | Hex | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dugout Navy | `#0B1F3A` | Primary surface, text, actions, and app icon |
| Optic Yellow | `#E8E337` | Small highlight, active state, XP, ball accent, or one focal action |
| Chalk | `#F7F5EF` | Main light background and text on Navy |
| Stitch Red | `#C8371F` | Softball sub-brand and real warnings/threats |
| Field Blue | `#1C3A66` | Hover and cards on Navy |
| Outfield Green | `#2E7D4F` | Success and strengths |
| Infield Clay | `#B5651D` | Opportunities and warm-neutral accents; avoid small white text on Clay |
| Slate | `#5B6B82` | Secondary text and borders |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Cards on Chalk |

Navy and Chalk should carry most of the interface. Light mode uses Chalk surfaces and Navy foregrounds. Dark mode swaps those roles while retaining the approved accents and readable semantic status colors.

### Contrast guidance

The palette's validated reference pairs include Chalk/Navy **15.15:1**, Optic Yellow/Navy **12.17:1**, Slate/Chalk **4.98:1**, Stitch Red/White **5.22:1**, and Outfield Green/White **5.05:1**. Optic Yellow/Chalk is only **1.24:1** and must not be used as a text pair. Re-check contrast when adding a new color or changing a semantic pairing.

## Typography

- **Headlines and wordmarks:** Barlow 800.
- **Uppercase labels and short emphases:** Barlow 700 with tracking.
- **Body and UI:** Inter variable, typically 400 for body and 600 for emphasis.
- Prefer the included WOFF2 assets and their accompanying OFL notices; do not hotlink font files from a third-party host.
- Use system sans-serif fallbacks when a consumer cannot bundle the fonts.

## Illustration and photography

`realball_flat.png` and `realball_transparent.png` are softball artwork. `softball-hitting.svg` and `softball-pitching.svg` are softball-specific pictograms. Treat these as supporting artwork, not as substitutes for the primary logo.

## Print

Coach Cards use a white paper surface with dark text, the primary wordmark, and softball-only pictograms. Always test printed output on the intended device; screen contrast does not guarantee printer contrast.
